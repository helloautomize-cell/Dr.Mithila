09 · Quick answers

Things people ask first.

Something else on your mind? Ask us directly

Is Dr. Mithila Desai a psychiatrist?

No. Dr. Desai is a clinical psychologist with a PhD. She helps through talking therapy and assessment and does not prescribe medicine.

Is what I say kept private?

Yes, with a few legal and safety limits that she explains at the first session.

Can I have sessions online?

Yes, by video at a booked time, just like a session in the room.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on what you bring and how often you can come. Progress is reviewed together.
