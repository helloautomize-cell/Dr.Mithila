## Privacy rules for this practice (please read first)

- **Never a real client in any photo**, not even from behind or blurred, and not even with consent. Being seen at a psychologist's is itself private.
- Use empty rooms, Dr. Desai alone, or models who have signed a release.
- No pictures of people in despair, heads in hands, pills, hospital beds or anything that suggests self-harm.
- No client files, notebooks, screens or appointment books visible in any shot.
- No award trophies in frame. Certificates may be shown plainly on the profile page only.
- Do not download photos from Practo, Justdial, Instagram or other sites. Send us the originals from your phone or photographer.
