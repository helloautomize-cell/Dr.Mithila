H1: About The Mental *Health*

Intro (answer-first): The Mental Health is the psychology practice of Dr. Mithila Desai, PhD, a clinical psychologist in Borivali West, Mumbai. It offers talking therapy and psychological assessment for adults, teenagers, children, couples and families, in person and online, and wellbeing programmes for schools and workplaces.

### Why the practice exists

Footer line from the old site, kept because it says it well `[CONFIRM: keep]`:
> "What mental health needs is more sunlight, more candor, and more unashamed conversations."

Dr. Desai chose psychology in the 1980s, at a time when few people in Mumbai spoke openly about mental health `[CONFIRM: wording]`. The practice grew from a simple idea: that people feel better when they are truly listened to, in a place where they do not feel judged. That idea still shapes every session.

### How we work

- **You are heard first.** The first session is about understanding you, not filling a form.
- **Plain words.** Dr. Desai explains what she notices and why she suggests something, so therapy never feels like a mystery.
- **Your pace.** You decide what to talk about and when. You can pause or stop at any time.
- **Evidence-based methods.** `Dr. Desai decides how to work with you after she has understood your concerns, your history and your goals. She draws on established talking therapies such as [CONFIRM: approaches she uses, in plain words; sources mention CBT, REBT, mindfulness and acceptance-based work], and explains each step as you go.`
- **Working with others when it helps.** With your consent, Dr. Desai can speak with your psychiatrist, doctor or your child's school.
- **Honest limits.** We tell you if another kind of help would suit you better, including a psychiatrist or a hospital service.
