### Intro (answer-first)

Workplace wellbeing programmes give employees confidential access to a psychologist and help managers respond well to stress, conflict and mental health concerns at work. The Mental Health, led by clinical psychologist Dr. Mithila Desai in Borivali West, Mumbai, offers employee counselling, manager guidance and workshops for organisations, in person and online. Dr. Desai has provided employee assistance programme (EAP) counselling for many years `[CONFIRM: years; partner organisations are not named]`.

### At a glance

- **For:** companies, offices, hospitals, schools and NGOs `[CONFIRM]`
- **Employee counselling:** confidential one-to-one sessions, in person or online
- **For managers and HR:** guidance on supporting a distressed employee
- **Workshops:** stress, burnout, sleep, communication, work-life balance
- **After difficult events:** support after an accident, death or crisis at work `[CONFIRM]`
- **Reporting:** only anonymous, group-level usage data; never individual details

### What an engagement can include

`[CONFIRM: what she offers]`
- One-to-one counselling sessions for employees (and family members, if agreed)
- Talks and workshops, in person or online
- Manager training on spotting distress and having supportive conversations
- Support after critical incidents
- Simple, privacy-safe reports on how the service is used

### Confidentiality

Employees' sessions are private. The organisation never receives names or session content. Usage reports are anonymous and grouped. Safety exceptions apply as for every client.

Send a request with your organisation, number of employees, location and what you are looking for. We reply within `[CONFIRM]` working days with a proposal. Fees are discussed directly.
