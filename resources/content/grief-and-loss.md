### Intro (answer-first)

Grief is the natural response to losing someone or something important. It can bring sadness, anger, guilt, numbness and physical tiredness, often in waves. At The Mental Health in Borivali West, clinical psychologist Dr. Mithila Desai offers a space to talk about loss and find a way to carry it, in person or online.

### At a glance

- **Losses we help with:** death of a family member or friend, miscarriage or stillbirth, suicide loss, divorce or separation, loss of health, job or home, a pet
- **Grief has no fixed timeline**
- **Therapy:** space to talk, remember, and adjust to life after the loss
- **Children grieve too**, in their own way

### When extra support may help

- Grief that feels as raw many months later as at the start
- Being unable to accept the death or talk about it
- Strong guilt or anger that does not ease
- Not being able to work, eat or sleep
- Feeling life has no meaning, or wanting to join the person who died
- A sudden or traumatic death, or a death by suicide

### How therapy helps

- Talking about the person and the loss freely, without being told to "move on"
- Understanding the mix of feelings, including relief, anger or guilt
- Handling rituals, anniversaries and family expectations
- Finding a way to keep a bond with the person while building life forward
- Supporting children in the family

### When to get urgent help

> **If you are in danger right now.** If you have hurt yourself, are thinking of ending your life, or feel you may act on thoughts of harming yourself or someone else, please do not wait for an appointment. Call **112**, go to the nearest hospital emergency department, or call **Tele-MANAS on 14416** or 1-800-891-4416. Tele-MANAS is the Government of India's free mental health helpline, open 24 hours, in many Indian languages. Messages to The Mental Health are answered during clinic hours.
