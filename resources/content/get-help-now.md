H1: If you need help *now*

Intro (large, first on the page): If you or someone near you is in immediate danger, call **112** now, or go to the nearest hospital emergency department. You can also call **Tele-MANAS on 14416** or 1-800-891-4416, the Government of India's free mental health helpline. It is open 24 hours, every day, in many Indian languages.

Buttons (large, stacked on mobile): [Call 112] [Call Tele-MANAS 14416]

Line: These helplines answer 24 hours a day. Messages to The Mental Health are answered during clinic hours.

### If you are thinking about ending your life

- You are not alone, and these feelings can pass, even when it does not seem so right now.
- Tell someone you trust today. You can say: "I am not okay and I need help."
- Move away from anything you could use to harm yourself, or ask someone to remove it.
- Stay with other people if you can.
- Call Tele-MANAS on 14416. Trained counsellors will listen and help you plan the next few hours.

### If you are worried about someone else

- Ask them directly: "Are you thinking about suicide?" Asking does not put the idea in their head. It often brings relief.
- Listen without arguing or lecturing.
- Do not leave them alone if you think they are in danger. Call 112.
- Help them call Tele-MANAS, or call together.
- Remove anything they could use to harm themselves, if it is safe to do so.

### If you have hurt yourself

If the injury is serious, if you have taken an overdose of any medicine or substance, or if you are not sure, call 112 or go to a hospital emergency department now, even if you feel fine.

### After the crisis

When you feel safer, ongoing support helps. You can [book a session](/contact/) with Dr. Mithila Desai, see your psychiatrist or doctor, or contact Tele-MANAS again for advice on local services.

### Other numbers

- Emergency (police, fire, ambulance): 112
- Ambulance in Maharashtra: 108
- Tele-MANAS: 14416 or 1-800-891-4416
- Women's helpline: 181
- Childline: 1098
