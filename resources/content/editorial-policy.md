H1: Editorial and clinical review *policy*

### Who writes and reviews our content
Every page and article is written or reviewed by Dr. Mithila Desai, PhD, clinical psychologist, RCI CRR No. `[CONFIRM]`. Some content is drafted by Automize Media Labs, our website partner, and is published only after Dr. Desai has reviewed and approved it.

### Our standards
- Plain, accurate language that respects the reader
- Based on current guidelines and research from recognised bodies, listed under "Sources"
- No promises of results, no comparisons with other professionals, no testimonials
- Safe messaging about suicide and self-harm, with helplines on every relevant page
- Clear about what a psychologist does and does not do

### Review dates
Each page shows "Last reviewed" with the date Dr. Desai last checked it. We aim to review all pages at least once a year, and sooner when guidelines change.

### AI-assisted content and images
We may use AI tools to help draft text, check readability or create illustrations. A person checks every piece, and Dr. Desai approves all clinical content. We never use AI to create images of real clients or to alter Dr. Desai's appearance without her written approval. Illustrations of people are not photos of real clients.

### Corrections
If you see an error, please email `[CONFIRM]`. We correct confirmed errors promptly and update the review date.

### Independence
We do not accept advertising or sponsorship, and we do not promote products or medicines.
