> **If you are in danger right now.** If you have hurt yourself, are thinking of ending your life, or feel you may act on thoughts of harming yourself or someone else, please do not wait for an appointment. Call **112**, go to the nearest hospital emergency department, or call **Tele-MANAS on 14416** or 1-800-891-4416. Tele-MANAS is the Government of India's free mental health helpline, open 24 hours, in many Indian languages. The Mental Health is not an emergency service and cannot respond to messages at all hours.

### Intro

Thoughts of suicide and urges to self-harm are signs of deep distress, not attention-seeking or weakness. They can be talked about safely, and they can ease with support. At The Mental Health in Borivali West, clinical psychologist Dr. Mithila Desai offers ongoing therapy for teenagers and adults who self-harm or have suicidal thoughts, alongside emergency and psychiatric care when needed.

### At a glance

- **Right now:** call 112 or Tele-MANAS 14416 if you are in danger
- **Ongoing therapy:** to understand the pain behind the thoughts and build safer ways to cope
- **Safety plan:** a written, personal plan for difficult moments
- **Psychiatrist:** often involved too, especially when risk is high
- **Families:** guidance for parents and partners

### If you self-harm

- You are not alone. Many people, especially teenagers and young adults, hurt themselves to cope with feelings that seem unbearable.
- Look after any wounds. If an injury is deep, will not stop bleeding, or you have swallowed anything harmful, go to a hospital emergency department now.
- Telling one trusted person is a strong first step.

### How therapy helps

- A safe, non-judging place to talk about what is happening
- A **safety plan**: warning signs, things that help, people to call, and how to make your surroundings safer
- Understanding what the urges do for you, and finding other ways to get the same relief
- Working on what lies underneath, such as depression, trauma, relationships or pressure
- Involving family or a psychiatrist when needed, with your knowledge

### For parents and partners

- Stay calm, listen, and ask directly about suicide. Asking does not put the idea in someone's head.
- Do not promise to keep it secret if their life is at risk.
- Remove or lock away medicines, sharp objects and other means at home.
- Help them get support, and get support for yourself too.
