### Intro (answer-first)

Career guidance helps students and adults choose a study or work path that suits their abilities, interests and personality. At The Mental Health in Borivali West, clinical psychologist Dr. Mithila Desai offers aptitude and interest testing with a guidance session for students after Class 8, 10 and 12, college students and adults thinking of a change.

### At a glance

- **Who:** students from Class 8 `[CONFIRM]` to college, and adults
- **What:** aptitude, interest and personality questionnaires, plus a guidance session
- **Parents:** invited to the feedback session
- **Result:** a report with suitable streams or paths and next steps
- **Also helps with:** exam stress and family disagreement about choices

### What happens

1. **Getting to know the student**: interests, marks, hobbies, worries and family expectations.
2. **Testing**: aptitude, interests and work style.
3. **Feedback**: a session with the student and parents to discuss options openly.
4. **Report and plan**: streams, courses or careers to explore, and how to find out more.

### FAQs

<div class="faq">
<details><summary>When is a good time for career guidance?<i></i></summary><p>Class 9 or 10 is a useful time, before choosing a stream. It also helps before choosing a college course, or at any age when you feel stuck in your work.</p></details>
<details><summary>My child and I disagree about their career. Can you help?<i></i></summary><p>Yes. Test results give an objective starting point, and a neutral person can help both sides listen. The aim is a choice the student can commit to.</p></details>
<details><summary>Does a test decide the career?<i></i></summary><p>No. Tests show strengths and interests. The decision stays with the student and family, made with better information.</p></details>
</div>
