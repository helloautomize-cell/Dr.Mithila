H1: Privacy *policy*

### 1. Who we are
{IDENTITY}
Dr. Mithila Desai decides why and how your personal data is used for The Mental Health. Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), this makes us the "data fiduciary".

### 2. What this policy covers
How we handle personal data you share with us through this website, by phone, WhatsApp or email, in sessions in person or online, and through organisations that arrange sessions for you. It applies to clients, parents and guardians, family members, and website visitors.

### 3. The information we collect

**Through this website**
- Booking form: your name, mobile number, email (optional), who the session is for, age, preferred way of meeting and times, city or country (optional), and for under-18s the parent or guardian's name and consent
- Organisation form: organisation name, contact person, email, mobile, and details of the request
- Technical data needed to run and secure the website. With your consent only, anonymous usage data through Google Analytics (section 9)

We ask you not to send details of your concerns through the website.

**In sessions**
- Identity and contact details, and an emergency contact
- Information you choose to share about your life, health and concerns
- Brief session notes, assessment responses, test results and reports
- Payment records (we do not store full card details)
- For online sessions: your location at the time of the session, for safety

We do not record sessions by audio or video `[CONFIRM]`.

### 4. Why we use your information
- To arrange, confirm and change appointments
- To provide therapy and assessments, and to plan your care
- To keep records needed for safe, consistent care and as the law requires
- To contact you about sessions, reports and payments
- To act in an emergency to protect life or safety
- To answer questions and complaints
- To keep the website working and secure
- To understand, anonymously, how the website is used (only with consent)

We do not sell your data. We do not use it for advertising. We do not send you promotional messages unless you ask.

### 5. Legal basis
We process your data:
- with your consent, given when you submit a form or start sessions; and
- for legitimate uses allowed under the DPDP Act, such as responding to a medical emergency or threat to life, and meeting legal duties.
You can withdraw consent at any time (section 10). Withdrawal does not affect care already given or records the law requires us to keep.

### 6. Children
For anyone under 18, we ask a parent or legal guardian to give verifiable consent, on the website form and at the first session. We use children's data only for their care. We do not track, profile or advertise to children. Dr. Desai explains to both the young person and the parent what is kept private within the family.

### 7. Confidentiality and when we share information
What you share in sessions is confidential. We share information only:
- **with your consent**, for example with your psychiatrist, doctor, school or employer, and only what is needed;
- **to protect life or safety**, when there is a serious and immediate risk to you or someone else;
- **to protect a child**, as required under the Protection of Children from Sexual Offences Act, 2012 and other law;
- **when the law requires it**, such as a court order.

Service providers who help us run the practice and the website may handle limited data on our behalf: messaging and email (WhatsApp, email), video calls `[CONFIRM: platform]`, secure cloud storage, website hosting, a form email service, a spam check service, payment `[CONFIRM]`, and, with consent, analytics. Some may store data outside India under their own security terms and in line with Indian law.

Organisations that arrange sessions for employees or students receive only anonymous, grouped information, never names or session content.

### 8. How long we keep information
- Session notes, assessments and reports: `[CONFIRM: e.g. at least 3 years after the last session; for children, until at least 3 years after they turn 18]`
- Booking requests that do not lead to a session: `[CONFIRM: e.g. 12 months]`, then deleted
- Analytics data (with consent): 2 months
After these periods, we delete or securely destroy the data.

### 9. Cookies and analytics
Essential cookies keep the website working, such as remembering your cookie choice. With your consent only, we use Google Analytics to see which pages are useful. No form content or health information is sent to analytics. Change your choice anytime with "Cookie settings" in the footer.

### 10. Your rights
Under the DPDP Act you can:
- ask what personal data we hold and how it is used
- ask us to correct or update it
- ask us to erase data we no longer need to keep by law
- withdraw consent
- nominate someone to exercise your rights if you die or cannot do so
- complain to us, and if not satisfied, to the Data Protection Board of India

Contact our privacy contact (section 12). We may need to confirm your identity.

### 11. How we protect information
- Paper records in locked storage `[CONFIRM]`
- Digital records in password-protected accounts with two-step verification `[CONFIRM]`
- Access limited to Dr. Desai and anyone directly involved in your care
- Encrypted website connections (HTTPS); website forms are sent by email and not stored in a database
If a data breach affects you, we will inform you and the Data Protection Board as the law requires.
