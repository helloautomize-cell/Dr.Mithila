### Intro (answer-first)

Schools and colleges see the first signs of stress, bullying and learning difficulties in young people. The Mental Health, led by clinical psychologist Dr. Mithila Desai in Borivali West, Mumbai, offers workshops for students, parents and teachers, and guidance for school counsellors, drawing on her long experience in school psychology `[CONFIRM]`.

### At a glance

- **Students:** exam stress, friendships, bullying and online safety, emotions, career choices
- **Parents:** talking with children, screens, study habits, spotting warning signs
- **Teachers:** recognising distress, learning difficulties and ADHD in class; supportive conversations
- **Format:** talks, interactive workshops, small groups `[CONFIRM]`
- **Languages:** English, Hindi `[CONFIRM]`

### How to request a session

Use the form with your school or college name, the audience, age group, number of participants, preferred dates, topics and language. We reply within `[CONFIRM]` working days.
