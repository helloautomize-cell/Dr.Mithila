### Intro (answer-first)

Many Indians living abroad prefer a therapist who understands their language, family life and culture. Clinical psychologist Dr. Mithila Desai of The Mental Health, Borivali West, Mumbai, offers online video sessions to Indians and people of Indian origin living outside India, at times planned around their time zone.

### Why people choose a therapist from India

- Speaking in your own language, or switching between languages
- Not having to explain joint families, in-law expectations, arranged marriage, or the pressure of being "the one who went abroad"
- Loneliness and identity questions after a move
- Worry about ageing parents in India
- Relationship strain across distance or cultures
- Pressure in a new job, study or visa situation

### How sessions work across time zones

1. Tell us your city and time zone when you book.
2. We offer slots that fall in your morning or evening `[CONFIRM]`.
3. The confirmation shows the time in both India time and your local time.
4. Daylight saving changes are checked twice a year.

### What online care from India can and cannot do

- Dr. Desai is registered as a clinical psychologist in India. Some countries and states have rules about receiving care from professionals registered elsewhere. You are responsible for checking whether this applies to you.
- She cannot arrange emergency care in your country. At the first session, she will ask you for a local emergency contact and the emergency number where you live.
- For medicine, you will need a doctor or psychiatrist licensed where you live.
