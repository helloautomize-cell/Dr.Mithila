### Intro (answer-first)

Family counselling brings family members together to understand a shared difficulty and find better ways to live with each other. At The Mental Health in Borivali West, clinical psychologist Dr. Mithila Desai offers family sessions and parenting support for families with children, teenagers or adult members, including joint families.

### At a glance

- **Who comes:** parents, children, grandparents or adult siblings, in different combinations
- **Common reasons:** conflict, change, a child's behaviour, illness or loss
- **Parenting support:** sessions for parents alone are welcome
- **Where:** Borivali West, or online for some sessions

### When family counselling can help

- Constant arguments, or members who no longer talk
- A teenager and parents who cannot discuss anything calmly
- Difficult behaviour in a child, and parents who disagree on how to respond
- A new marriage, a new baby, a move, or a parent moving in
- Joint family tensions and boundaries
- Separation or divorce, and helping children through it
- A family member's illness, disability, mental health condition or substance use
- A death in the family

### Parenting support

Parents can come on their own for help with:
- Routines, screen time and sleep
- Responding to tantrums, defiance or aggression
- Supporting an anxious or withdrawn child
- Talking to children about difficult topics
- Parenting a teenager while staying connected
- Co-parenting after separation

### What happens in sessions

1. **Hearing everyone.** Each person gets a turn to speak and be heard.
2. **Looking at patterns.** Dr. Desai helps the family see what keeps a problem going, rather than who is to blame.
3. **Small changes.** The family agrees on practical steps at home.
4. **Different combinations.** Some sessions include everyone, some only parents or only siblings.

### When to get urgent help

> **If you are in danger right now.** If you have hurt yourself, are thinking of ending your life, or feel you may act on thoughts of harming yourself or someone else, please do not wait for an appointment. Call **112**, go to the nearest hospital emergency department, or call **Tele-MANAS on 14416** or 1-800-891-4416. Tele-MANAS is the Government of India's free mental health helpline, open 24 hours, in many Indian languages. Messages to The Mental Health are answered during clinic hours.

### FAQs

<div class="faq">
<details><summary>Does everyone in the family have to come?<i></i></summary><p>No. It often helps to have the people most involved, but sessions can start with whoever is willing. Change in one or two people often helps the whole family. Dr. Desai will suggest who to include as things progress.</p></details>
<details><summary>Can grandparents join?<i></i></summary><p>Yes. In many Indian families grandparents play a big role in raising children, and including them can make plans easier to follow at home.</p></details>
<details><summary>Is family counselling only for big problems?<i></i></summary><p>No. Many families come during a change, such as a new baby, a move or a child starting college, to adjust well before small problems grow.</p></details>
<details><summary>What if a family member has a mental health condition?<i></i></summary><p>Family sessions can help everyone understand the condition, reduce blame and conflict, and support recovery. If medicine is involved, Dr. Desai can work alongside the psychiatrist with consent.</p></details>
</div>
