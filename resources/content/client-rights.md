H1: Client rights and *responsibilities*

Based on the Mental Healthcare Act, 2017, the Charter of Patients' Rights (Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, 2019) and professional ethics for psychologists.

### Your rights
1. **Respect and dignity**, without discrimination on grounds of religion, caste, gender, sexual orientation, disability, age or background.
2. **Information** about how Dr. Desai works, what is suggested and why, and other options, including seeing a psychiatrist.
3. **Consent.** Nothing is done without your agreement. You may ask questions, decline or stop at any time.
4. **Confidentiality**, with the limits explained at the first session.
5. **Access to your information**, including a copy of reports about you.
6. **Fees told in advance**, before you confirm a session.
7. **A second opinion** from another professional at any time.
8. **Complaints**, heard fairly without affecting your care.
9. **Children's rights**: young people are heard and involved in decisions as fits their age.

### Your responsibilities
1. Give honest information, including any medicine you take and other professionals you see.
2. Arrive on time, or tell us early if you need to cancel.
3. Respect the privacy of other clients you may see at the practice.
4. Do not record sessions without written agreement.
5. Tell us if you are not finding sessions helpful, so we can talk about it.
6. In a crisis, use emergency services (112, Tele-MANAS 14416).
7. Pay fees as agreed.

### Complaints
Speak to Dr. Desai directly, or email `[CONFIRM]`. We aim to acknowledge within `[CONFIRM]` working days and respond fully within `[CONFIRM]` days. If you are not satisfied, you may contact the Rehabilitation Council of India.
