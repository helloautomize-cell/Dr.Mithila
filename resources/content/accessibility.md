H1: Accessibility *statement*

### Our aim
We want everyone to be able to use this website, including people who use screen readers, keyboards, magnification or voice control, and people with anxiety or attention difficulties who prefer calm, simple pages.

### What we have done
- Built to Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, level AA
- Clear headings, simple language and short paragraphs
- Body text at least 17 pixels, with an A / A+ text-size switch
- Colour contrast of at least 4.5:1
- Every function works with a keyboard, with visible focus
- Text alternatives for images
- No flashing content; animations stop for people who choose reduced motion
- Moving carousels have pause buttons
- Forms with clear labels and error messages
