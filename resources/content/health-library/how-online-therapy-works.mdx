## The process, step by step

Online therapy is the same psychological work as in-person therapy, delivered over video instead of in the same room. The steps are ordinary: you book a session, receive a private meeting link, join at the agreed time, and talk with your psychologist for the session length, usually the same hour you would spend in the clinic.

The first few minutes of a first session are orientation: checking you can see and hear each other, agreeing what to do if the connection drops, and covering confidentiality and its limits. Then the session begins exactly as any first session does.

## How privacy actually works

On the psychologist's side, sessions use secure meeting links, they sit in a private space, and sessions are not recorded. On yours, the practical steps matter most: headphones, a closed door, and letting people at home know you are on a confidential call. If a platform or therapist cannot explain their privacy setup clearly, ask before you book.

## After the first session

Later sessions feel less formal. You join the same way, review your week, and keep working on whatever you are building together: understanding patterns, practising new responses, handling specific situations differently. If the direction ever feels unclear, say so; adjusting course is part of the work.
