### Intro (answer-first)

A habit becomes a problem when it is hard to control and starts to harm your health, relationships, work or money. At The Mental Health in Borivali West, clinical psychologist Dr. Mithila Desai offers counselling for people who want to cut down or stop alcohol, tobacco, cannabis, gaming, gambling, phone use and other habits, and support for their families.

### At a glance

- **Habits we can help with:** alcohol, tobacco and nicotine, cannabis, gaming, betting, phone and social media, pornography, shopping `[CONFIRM: list]`
- **Counselling:** understanding triggers, building motivation, planning change, preventing relapse
- **Medical care:** needed for withdrawal from alcohol and some drugs (see below)
- **Families:** support for family members, even if the person is not ready

### Important: medical safety

Stopping alcohol suddenly after heavy daily drinking, or stopping some sedative medicines, can cause dangerous withdrawal, including seizures. Please speak to a doctor or psychiatrist before stopping. Psychologists do not provide medical detox. Dr. Desai can work alongside your doctor or a de-addiction centre.

### Signs a habit has become a problem

- Using more, or for longer, than you planned
- Trying to cut down and not managing
- Cravings or restlessness without it
- Hiding it from family
- Missing work, study or responsibilities
- Money problems, arguments or health effects

### How counselling helps

- Looking honestly at what the habit does for you and what it costs
- Finding your reasons for change
- Identifying triggers: people, places, feelings, times of day
- Practical plans for cravings and difficult moments
- Rebuilding sleep, routine and relationships
- Planning for slips, which are common and can be learnt from

### For families

Living with someone's addiction is exhausting. Sessions can help you set limits, stop covering up, look after yourself and encourage help without constant fights.

### When to get urgent help

> **If you are in danger right now.** If you have hurt yourself, are thinking of ending your life, or feel you may act on thoughts of harming yourself or someone else, please do not wait for an appointment. Call **112**, go to the nearest hospital emergency department, or call **Tele-MANAS on 14416** or 1-800-891-4416. Tele-MANAS is the Government of India's free mental health helpline, open 24 hours, in many Indian languages. Messages to The Mental Health are answered during clinic hours.
