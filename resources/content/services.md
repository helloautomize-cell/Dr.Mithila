H1: How we can *help*

Intro: The Mental Health offers talking therapy and psychological assessment with clinical psychologist Dr. Mithila Desai in Borivali West, Mumbai, and online. Start with the way of working that fits your situation, or with the concern you would like help with.

### Ways we work
Glass cards: Individual therapy · Child and teen therapy · Couples counselling · Family counselling · Online therapy · Online therapy for Indians abroad

### Concerns we help with
Glass cards: Anxiety and panic · Depression and low mood · OCD · Stress, anger and burnout · Trauma · Self-esteem and body image · Grief and loss · Habits, addiction and dependence · Self-harm and suicidal thoughts · Sleep problems · LGBTQIA+ affirmative counselling

### Assessments
Glass cards: Psychological assessment · ADHD, learning and neurodiversity · Career guidance and aptitude testing

### For organisations
Glass cards: Workplace wellbeing and EAP · Schools and colleges

### What we do not offer

So you can find the right help quickly:
- **Medicine.** Psychologists do not prescribe. Please see a psychiatrist for medicine.
- **Emergency care.** If you or someone else is in danger, call 112 or Tele-MANAS 14416, or go to the nearest hospital emergency department.
- **Hospital or residential care**, including medical detox.
- **Disability certificates**, which are issued by government medical boards.
